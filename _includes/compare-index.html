Compare API providers and markets

Two APIs.io tools answer the question every API decision comes down to: how does this compare? One works on vendors, the other on whole markets. Both score on the same rubric, the Kin Score plus agent readiness, so their answers line up with each other.

compare_providers

Two vendors, head to head

Composite and band, the leader on each facet, which artifacts each publishes, and what each costs to get into, quoted from their own pricing pages. {{ SC.providers.size }} worked examples, from Stripe vs Adyen to OpenAI vs Anthropic.

 compare_cohorts

Two markets, one rubric

Distributions, band splits, facet averages and adoption rates for any two tags, industries, regions or VC portfolios, plus a check on whether the gap reflects the market or our own coverage. {{ SC.cohorts.size }} worked examples.
