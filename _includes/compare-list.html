Compare two API markets on one rubric

Is payments further along than banking? Does Europe publish better APIs than North America? A written report covers one market at a time. compare_cohorts puts any two tags, industries, regions, areas or VC portfolios side by side: both distributions, and the delta on the Kin Score, agent readiness, every facet and every adoption rate.

It also tells you when not to trust the gap. When the higher-scoring cohort is also the one we enriched more deeply, depth_confounded is true, and each comparison below shows that warning before its narrative. Here are {{ SC.cohorts.size }} real results.
